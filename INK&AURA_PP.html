Privacy Policy

App: INK & AURA Wheel & Tyre Tools  ·  Last updated: 25 November 2026

The short version

  • We do not collect, store on a server, sell or share your personal information.
  • The app works offline. It has no internet access.
  • Everything you enter stays on your phone or tablet.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • You can delete all your data at any time with Reset app in Settings.

Who we are

INK & AURA Wheel & Tyre Tools ("the app") is provided by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY] ("we", "us"). If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at samralsowani@gmail.com].

What the app does with your information

The app lets you do tyre and wheel calculations, record tyre inspections, convert pressures, build quotes and keep workshop records. To do this you may type in information such as:

  • Tyre and wheel sizes, tread depths, DOT codes and pressures
  • Customer names and phone numbers
  • Vehicle registrations, makes and models
  • Quote details, prices, notes and your workshop name

This information is saved only on your own device, inside the app's private storage. We never receive it, and we cannot see it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal data on any server.
  • We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, crash-tracking or any other third-party tracking tools.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data. The app does not ask for any Android permissions.

Sharing a quote

If you tap Share on a quote, the app hands the quote text to Android's share menu. You choose which app (for example email or messaging) receives it. That is your choice and happens only when you tap Share. After that, the app you chose handles the text under its own privacy policy.

Customer details you enter

If you use the app for your business and enter details about your customers, you are responsible for looking after that information and for following the privacy laws that apply to you (for example, telling customers how you use their details). The app keeps the data on your device only. Please keep your device protected with a screen lock.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your data stays on your device until you delete it.
  • You can delete individual records inside the app.
  • To remove everything, open Settings → Reset app. This erases all records, saved pressure references and settings.
  • Uninstalling the app also removes its stored data from your device.
  • The app is set up so that its data is not included in Android cloud backups or device-to-device transfers.

Children

The app is a workshop tool and is not aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold any of your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You have full control of your data on your own device. If you have a question or concern, please contact us using the email above.

Important note about results

Calculations and verdicts in the app are screening aids only. Always follow the vehicle and tyre manufacturer's specifications and have work checked by a qualified technician.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If the app ever starts collecting data, we will update this policy and the app's store listing before it does.
