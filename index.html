Our Story

Driven by Detail, Finished in Ink

INK & AURA began with a simple belief: wheels are the most honest part of a car. They show how it's driven, how it's kept, and how much its owner cares.

From our workshop at Radleigh House, 1 Golf Road, we combine precision engineering with a genuinely obsessive finish standard — supplying, fitting and maintaining wheels for drivers across the United Kingdom.
